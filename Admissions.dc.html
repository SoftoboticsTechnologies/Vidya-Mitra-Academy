Quick Answers Before You Apply

A few common questions students ask before starting their admission enquiry.

Is there a fee for the admission enquiry itself?

No. Sending an enquiry, speaking with our team, and getting guidance on courses is free and carries no obligation.

Can I apply if I have a gap in my education?

Yes. Many students who come to us have taken a break from studies. Tell us where you left off and we will help you find a suitable way to continue.

How long does the admission process usually take?

This depends on the program and institution you choose. Once you enquire, we will give you a clear idea of the timeline for your specific case.

Can I choose between regular and correspondence formats?

Where both formats are available for a program, yes. Let us know your circumstances and we will point you to the option that fits.

Need Help Choosing a Course?

Choosing between streams and formats is often the hardest part. If you are unsure whether to take a regular or correspondence program, or which qualification your previous study leads into, speak to us before you decide.
