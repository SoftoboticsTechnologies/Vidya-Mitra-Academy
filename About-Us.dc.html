About Us

A Second Chance at Education, and a Clear Path Forward

Vidya Mitra Academy is an education focused institution providing guidance and access to a wide range of regular and correspondence programs.

Who We Are

Vidya Mitra Academy helps students explore educational opportunities across pharmacy, engineering, undergraduate, postgraduate, pre university and school level programs. We work with each student to understand where they are in their education, what they want to achieve, and which academic route makes that possible.

The academy was founded on a simple belief: every student deserves a second chance and a clear path toward their goals, regardless of their starting point. What began as a small guidance centre helping students navigate confusing admission processes has grown into a trusted academy offering both regular and correspondence courses across pharmacy, engineering, arts, commerce and science streams.

Over the years, under the leadership of Chairman Mr. Darshan Kuruvatti, Vidya Mitra Academy has helped many students restart their education, switch streams, complete incomplete degrees, and build careers they once thought were out of reach. Today the academy continues to grow with the same founding purpose: making quality education accessible, flexible and achievable for every student.

Our Vision

To make education accessible to every student who wants it, so that a break in study, a change of mind, or a difficult year never becomes the end of the road. We want each student who comes to us to leave with a realistic academic plan and better career opportunities ahead of them.

Our Mission

"We Give You a Rebirth of Your Education."

We give students a rebirth of their education by opening up programs, formats and pathways that fit the life they are living now. It is never too late to continue your education, upgrade your qualification, or take a new career direction.

Our Approach

Five things we do for every student who contacts the academy.

Student Focused Guidance

We start with your background and your goals, not with a list of what we would like to fill.

Multiple Course Options

Pharmacy, engineering, UG, PG, PUC and school level programs, in regular and correspondence formats.

Academic Pathway Support

Clarity on what follows what, so each course you take leads somewhere you actually want to go.

Admission Assistance

Career Oriented Choices

Course decisions made with the work you want to do afterwards kept firmly in view.
