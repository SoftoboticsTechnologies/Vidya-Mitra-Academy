About the Academy

Go beyond a single stream with Vidya Mitra Academy

The academy was founded on a simple belief: every student deserves a second chance and a clear path toward their goals, regardless of their starting point. What began as a small guidance centre has grown into a trusted academy offering regular and correspondence courses across pharmacy, engineering, arts, commerce and science.

Under the leadership of Darshan Kuruvatti, Vidya Mitra Academy has helped students restart their education, switch streams and complete degrees they had left unfinished.

What We Offer

At Vidya Mitra Academy, we provide a wide range of educational programs designed to help students explore diverse academic and career opportunities. Our programs cover Pharmacy, Engineering, Undergraduate, Postgraduate, PUC, SSLC and other educational courses, giving students the flexibility to choose an academic path that suits their goals. Whether you are looking to begin your education, continue your studies, or explore a new career direction, we aim to provide the right guidance and opportunities to help you move forward with confidence. Your education can have a new beginning, and your future can have a new direction.
