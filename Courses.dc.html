Pharmacy Courses

Pharmacy

Diploma, degree and doctoral level pharmacy programs for students planning a career in pharmaceutical practice, retail and hospital pharmacy, or industry.

D.Pharm

Diploma in Pharmacy, an entry route into pharmacy practice and a foundation for further study in the field.

B.Pharm

Bachelor of Pharmacy, a full degree covering pharmaceutical sciences, available in regular and correspondence formats.

Pharm.D

Doctor of Pharmacy, a clinically oriented program for students aiming at patient care and hospital settings.

Engineering Courses

Engineering

Technical programs at diploma and degree level, in regular and correspondence formats where applicable.

Engineering Programs

Regular and correspondence engineering degree options. Contact us to discuss which branches are available to you.

Diploma & Polytechnic

Diploma and polytechnic engineering programs where applicable, a practical route into technical work or further study.

Undergraduate Programs

Undergraduate

Bachelor degrees across arts, commerce, science and computer applications.

B.A.

Bachelor of Arts, a broad humanities degree with flexible study formats.

B.Com.

Bachelor of Commerce, suited to students heading into accounts, finance and business roles.

B.Sc.

Bachelor of Science, a science degree that also opens routes into postgraduate study.

BCA

Bachelor of Computer Applications, for students moving into software and IT work.

Postgraduate Programs

Postgraduate

Master's level programs for graduates who want to specialise or strengthen their qualifications.

M.A.

Master of Arts, a postgraduate humanities degree following a bachelor's qualification.

M.Sc.

Master of Science, advanced study in a science subject for graduates of relevant streams.

MCA

Master of Computer Applications, a postgraduate route for students building a career in computing.

Pre University

PUC

Two year pre university programs, the step between school and a degree course.

2 Year PUC, Science

The science stream at pre university level, the usual foundation for pharmacy, engineering and B.Sc. routes.

2 Year PUC, Arts

The arts stream at pre university level, leading into B.A. and related undergraduate programs.

2 Year PUC, Commerce

The commerce stream at pre university level, a common step towards B.Com. and business studies.

School Level Education

SSLC & Other Programs

School level qualifications for students who need to complete this stage before moving on.

SSLC

Secondary school level education, the qualification that opens the door to PUC and diploma courses.
