FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not answered here, contact the academy and we will respond directly.

What courses are available?

Vidya Mitra Academy provides guidance and access to pharmacy, engineering, undergraduate, postgraduate, pre university and school level programs, in regular and correspondence formats. The Courses page lists each category in full.

Which pharmacy courses are available?

D.Pharm, the Diploma in Pharmacy, B.Pharm, the Bachelor of Pharmacy, and Pharm.D.

Do you provide admission guidance?

Yes. We help students choose a suitable course, understand what the program requires, and work through the admission process step by step.

What undergraduate courses are available?

B.A., B.Com., B.Sc. and BCA.

What postgraduate courses are available?

M.A., M.Sc. and MCA.

Are PUC programs available?

Yes. Two year PUC programs are available in Science, Arts and Commerce.

Is SSLC related education available?

Yes. SSLC and other applicable school level programs are supported. Contact the academy for the full list.

Are engineering courses available in correspondence format?

Engineering programs are offered in regular and correspondence formats, and diploma or polytechnic engineering programs where applicable. Contact us to confirm what is available for your case.

How can I enquire about a course?

Use the Enquire Now button in the header, fill in the enquiry form on the Home or Contact page, or message us on WhatsApp. Include the course you are interested in and a short note about your education so far.
